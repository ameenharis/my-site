2026

Forecasting hurricane weather a week ahead

One PyTorch neural network that forecasts rainfall, wind speed, wind direction and surface pressure seven days ahead from 1.4 million rows of NASA data. Wind direction is circular (359° sits next to 1°), so it's encoded as sine and cosine.

Wind speed forecast within 1.6 m/s (RMSE) on held-out data.

Fun fact: 359° and 1° are neighbours. It took the model a sine and a cosine to believe me.

2026

Which mushrooms are safe to eat?

A deliberately shallow decision tree that turns 22 physical features into a few rules a person could follow. Calling a poisonous mushroom edible is far worse than the reverse, so the model is judged on how many poisonous ones it catches.

98% accuracy and 99.7% of poisonous mushrooms caught. Odour does most of the work.

The model's main advice: if it smells foul, don't eat it. Your nose already knew.

Which film genre makes money?

An analysis of 3,789 films to recommend a genre for a small studio's next release, comparing median profit margin and the share of films that turned a profit across the 11 largest genres.

Horror came out on top: 63% of horror films made a profit, with a median margin of 30.5%.

Scary films, scarily good returns.
