Project 02 · 2026

Forecasting hurricane weather a week ahead

One PyTorch neural network that forecasts rainfall, wind speed, wind direction and surface pressure seven days ahead from 1.4 million rows of NASA data. Wind direction is circular (359° sits next to 1°), so it's encoded as sine and cosine before training.

RESULT › wind speed forecast within 1.6 m/s (RMSE) on held-out data

359° and 1° are neighbours. It took the model a sine and a cosine to believe me.

Project 03 · BTech final year · 2022

Real-time driver drowsiness detection

A webcam system that watches a driver's eyes and mouth, sounds an alarm when they look drowsy, and emails an emergency contact if the alarm keeps going off. Two InceptionV3 transfer-learning models (eyes and yawns), OpenCV for face detection, and a Flask form for the emergency contact. Built with a teammate.

Its toughest test subjects were final-year students.

Project 04 · 2026

Which mushrooms are safe to eat?

A deliberately shallow decision tree that turns 22 physical features into a few rules a person could follow. Calling a poisonous mushroom edible is far worse than the reverse, so the model is judged on how many poisonous ones it catches.

Caught 99.7% of poisonous mushrooms. Odour did most of the work.

The model's main advice: if it smells foul, don't eat it. Your nose already knew.
